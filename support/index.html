Boxtide - Support

Boxtide is a calm container-sorting puzzle by HavivStudios. Containers roll in at the gate one at a time; you choose the stack each one goes to, and load the ships.

Contact: havivgamestudios@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we answer within a few days.

My purchase did not arrive, or I reinstalled the game

Open Settings > Purchases > Restore in the game, or Restore purchases on the Store sheet. Remove ads comes back on any phone signed in to the same store account, and the Starter pack stays marked as bought. Coins, cranes and the other packs are kept in your saved progress.

Keeping my progress

Your progress is saved on your phone. When the phone is signed in to its cloud save (iCloud on iPhone), it is also kept there and comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone with the same account.

Ads and privacy

Ads between levels stop with Remove ads; rewarded ads are always your choice. Your ad privacy choices can be changed at any time in Settings > Ad privacy > Choices. The privacy policy explains what the game and its partners collect, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

Reminders

Boxtide asks before it sends any reminder. Turn them off in Settings > Notifications, or in your phone's settings.
